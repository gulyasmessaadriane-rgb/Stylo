# ✨ Stylo – A Morning Lifestyle App

Stylo is your personal morning assistant. When you wake up, Stylo helps you decide:
- **What to wear** 👗
- **How you feel** 💭
- **What your day looks like** 📅
- **How you want to show up** 🌟
- **What's happening with family** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
- **What you want to create** 🎨
- **What you want to track** 📊

It's a closet, a planner, a mood mirror, a creative space, and a gentle companion all in one.

---

## 🌅 The Morning Feeling

Stylo is designed for how people actually feel in the morning:
- Low energy
- Sensitive to brightness
- Craving comfort
- Wanting motivation & clarity
- Wanting something gentle but inviting

The entire design philosophy—colors, shapes, words, interactions—centers on this **morning comfort + morning motivation**.

---

## 🎨 The Design System

### Color Palette
- **Comfort**: Peach, cream, champagne → safe, calm
- **Energy**: Coral → tap, choose, act
- **Freshness**: Mint → clean, new, healthy
- **Importance**: Soft gold → highlights achievements
- **Clarity**: Warm charcoal → readable, friendly text

### Design Principles
- Rounded corners (no sharp edges)
- Soft shadows & gentle spacing
- Pill-shaped buttons
- Warm, soft, friendly aesthetic
- One main purpose per screen
- One main action per screen
- One main color mood per screen

---

## 🧱 The 10 Main Sections

### 1. **Home** (Morning Hub)
First thing you see when you wake up. Shows today's outfit, mood, events, and quick access buttons.

### 2. **Closet**
Organized, clean wardrobe browsing. All your clothing items live here.

### 3. **Stylist**
AI fashion assistant. Suggests outfits based on weather, mood, and calendar. Learn why each outfit was chosen.

### 4. **Avatar**
Your "morning identity" – a mirror with good lighting. Express your mood and see yourself.

### 5. **Chat**
Talk to AI or family. Warm, safe, friendly conversation space.

### 6. **Sketch**
Creative space. Clean, open, like a fresh notebook page.

### 7. **Calendar**
Planner. Calm, simple, not stressful. Track your days.

### 8. **Tracker**
Gentle habit tracking. Mood, outfits, habits, progress – without judgment.

### 9. **Deals**
Helpful, smart recommendations. Not salesy.

### 10. **Store / Sponsor**
Premium, elegant, boutique-like shopping experience.

## 🚀 Getting Started

3. **Review the design system** → `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`
4. **Start building screens** → `src/screens/`
5. **Test the morning experience** → Ask: "Does this feel good when you wake up?"
